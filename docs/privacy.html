Kaiditya — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Kaiditya is a single-player game. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • No network access: the game works fully offline.
  • No in-app purchases. In-game coins are earned by playing and have no real-world value.

Game progress (levels unlocked, coins, stars, settings) is saved only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Because no data is collected, the game is suitable for children and families.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/adityakinifr/kaiditya/issues.
