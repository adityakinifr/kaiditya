Kaiditya — Support

How to play: drag on the left side of the screen to move. Tap HIDE to blend in, DASH to zoom, SHIELD to block, and the big ACTION button to talk, grapple, and more. Sneak past the minions' vision cones to finish each mission.

Stars: finish the mission, don't get spotted, and collect every coin to earn all three.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/adityakinifr/kaiditya/issues.
